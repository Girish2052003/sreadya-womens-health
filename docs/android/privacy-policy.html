Sreva Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Core privacy rule: Account-free Sreva and account-based Sreva are equal first-class experiences. Core health tools remain available without an account. Optional account continuity can synchronize client-encrypted data, but Sreva does not operate a readable reproductive-health database.

Account-free and local core

You can use Sreva without an account. Period and cycle tracking, logging, predictions, insights, reminders, reports, privacy controls and encrypted backup remain local-first core health features. When sync is off, those records stay in the application-controlled local vault unless you deliberately share, export or integrate them with a platform health store. Disabling sync does not disable local Sreva.

Data Sreva can store locally

Depending on what you choose to log, Sreva can store period dates and flow, symptoms, mood and pain, temperature, weight, exercise, water, medications and supplements, fertility observations, pregnancy or ovulation test observations, sexual-activity observations, private notes, app preferences, predictions and reminder settings. These records are optional unless needed for a feature you choose to use.

Account and encrypted continuity

If you enable optional continuity, your health content is encrypted before leaving an authorized device. The synchronization service receives ciphertext, wrapped key/recovery material and only the minimum operational metadata needed for account/device authorization and synchronization. That metadata can include opaque account, device, vault, object or event identifiers, ciphertext size, protocol/version counters, synchronization timestamps, public device-verification material, and authorization or revocation state. Sreva infrastructure does not possess the health-vault decryption key.

Account recovery and trusted devices

Account access and old-vault decryption are separate. Passkeys and account sessions can authorize the account. If a release enables verified email or SMS recovery, email or SMS can help recover account identity but cannot by itself decrypt the old health vault. A trusted device can approve a new device, and the independent recovery key can restore vault continuity when trusted devices are lost. Losing every trusted device and the recovery key can make the previous encrypted vault unrecoverable; Sreva has no hidden administrator recovery key.

Developer collection, diagnostics and analytics

Sreva does not use advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics, remote session replay or developer health-payload telemetry. Sync infrastructure may process the ciphertext and minimum operational metadata described above, but it must not receive readable menstrual history. User-generated diagnostic reports are designed to contain operational status rather than period dates, symptoms, notes, sexual activity, fertility history, pregnancy state or other health payloads.

Health Connect and Apple Health

Platform health integration is optional and permission-scoped. Sreva requests access only after you choose it and only for supported categories required by the feature. Imported records retain source information to reduce duplicates. You can revoke platform permissions in operating-system settings. Platform health stores are separate integrations under their platform providers' rules.

Sharing, reports and backups

A doctor report, partner summary, QR code, CycleVault or other export leaves the app only after an explicit user action. Private categories are excluded from partner sharing by default and sensitive report categories require explicit selection. CycleVault backups are encrypted locally with a user-chosen passphrase; the destination and retained copies remain under the user's control.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you edit or delete them, wipe Sreva data, uninstall the app, or restore another validated backup. In account mode, deleting the account removes server-side account/device state, wrapped recovery material and synchronized ciphertext that Sreva controls. Sreva cannot remotely erase former device copies, screenshots, user-created exports, CycleVault backups or data already sent to another destination. Those copies must be deleted where they are stored. Revoking a device blocks future authorized sync but does not claim retroactive erasure of its old local data.

Email, SMS and other providers

Email or SMS verification providers are used only if those channels are enabled for a release. If enabled, they may process an address or phone number and delivery/security metadata under their own terms. App stores, operating systems, notification services, Health Connect, Apple Health and user-selected share destinations may also process technical or user-directed data independently of Sreva.

Security

Sreva uses encrypted local storage, device-protected key storage where available, optional app locking, private notification modes, authenticated encrypted backups, reviewed E2EE continuity, signed-device authorization and release-time privacy/dependency checks. Browser protections differ from native Keychain/Keystore protections and are not represented as equivalent secure hardware. No security mechanism can make a lost or compromised device risk-free.

Children and age eligibility

Sreva's initial release is intended for adults. The publisher should not market it as a child-directed service without a separate privacy, legal and product review.

Changes to this policy

Material privacy changes require reviewed application and documentation updates. A sync-enabled release must keep the public policy, in-app policy, Privacy Center and applicable store declarations aligned with the exact shipping behavior.

Contact

The current developer and publisher contact details are provided in Sreva's official store listing when that distribution channel is active. Privacy and security questions can use the verified publisher contact channel provided there.
