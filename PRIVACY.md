# Sreva Privacy Architecture

Sreva's core promise is simple: **the user's reproductive-health data is not a developer database.**

Core cycle data is stored locally on the user's device. Prediction, insight generation, reminder planning, doctor-report generation, and natural-language command parsing are designed to operate locally.

Platform health integrations are optional and permission-scoped. User-controlled exports leave the app only after an explicit share/export action.

Sreva must not claim that no third party ever processes any device metadata: Apple/Google may process store downloads, operating-system backups, platform health records, or user-selected share destinations independently of Sreva. The product claim is narrower and enforceable: **Sreva's operator does not receive or maintain a reproductive-health database for core app operation.**
